## The goal

Grow organic visibility for an AI training academy's courses in a crowded education market.

## What I do

- Plan content around the course and career searches students actually make
- Lead the SEO and content team: assigning tasks, tracking daily progress and reviewing work before it goes live
- Train interns in SEO, front-end development, blog writing and using AI tools properly for coding

## Results

BlackBox Learning has a content plan built around the course and career searches its students make, and a team that works through it every day. Tasks are assigned and tracked daily, and nothing goes live until I've reviewed it. Interns learn SEO, front-end development and blog writing by doing the real work, not from a separate course.
