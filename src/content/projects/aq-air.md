## The brief

Present a concept product, an indoor air-quality monitor that tracks CO₂, PM2.5, VOCs, gas and temperature, in a way that makes complex readings feel simple and trustworthy.

## What I built

- A scroll-driven product story in vanilla JavaScript using IntersectionObserver and requestAnimationFrame
- Smooth scrolling with Lenis, a custom preloader, reveal-on-scroll sections and a draggable use-case carousel with mouse and touch support
- An animated live air-quality panel that explains the product at a glance
- A responsive layout for desktop, tablet and mobile, with reduced-motion support

## Why no framework

The page is mostly one long, animated story. Plain JavaScript kept it light and gave me full control over timing, without shipping a framework the page didn't need.

## What I learned

Animation is easy to overdo. The hardest part was deciding what to leave out, so every movement on the page helps explain the product instead of just decorating it. Mapping out the scroll story before writing any code saved me from rebuilding sections later.
