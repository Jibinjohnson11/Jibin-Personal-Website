## The goal

Help a healthcare brand show up for the treatments and locations its patients search for.

## What I did

- Planned keyword targets around treatments and locations, focusing on high-intent searches
- Ran the content pipeline from drafting through client review and approval to publication
- Coordinated link-building work across blogs, articles and other platforms
- Tracked progress in Google Search Console and Google Analytics and reported it to the client

## Results

Serene Derma now has a keyword plan built around its treatments and the locations it serves, so every new page has a clear search to target. Each piece of content went through client review before it was published, which kept the medical details accurate. Link building ran alongside the content, and the client got regular reports from Search Console and Analytics showing what had changed and what we'd work on next.
