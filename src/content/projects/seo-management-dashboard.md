## The problem

Running SEO campaigns meant tracking drafts, approvals and team tasks across spreadsheets and messages. More time went into coordination than into the SEO work itself.

## What I'm building

A Django application that brings the whole workflow into one place:

- A content approval flow that follows each draft from writing through review to publication
- Campaign and task tracking, so everyone can see project status at a glance
- Reporting views that summarise progress for managers and clients

## Tech decisions

Django gave me authentication, an admin panel and a solid ORM out of the box, so I could focus on the workflow itself. PostgreSQL handles the relational data: campaigns, team members, content items and their approval history.

## Status

I'm still building this. I started with content approvals because that's where our team lost the most time, and I'm adding features as I use it on real campaigns.
